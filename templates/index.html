How this works & limitations
  • No email is sent. Verification does an SMTP handshake (EHLO → MAIL FROM → RCPT TO) and stops before DATA.
  • Port 25 may be blocked on your network (common for residential ISPs and many cloud hosts). If every result comes back error, that's almost certainly why.
  • Catch-all domains accept mail for any address — those results are marked and are not trustworthy.
  • Accept-then-bounce providers (Gmail, Microsoft 365, many corporate servers) often return success for almost anything at this stage — treat "valid" there with skepticism.
  • Be respectful: keep the delay reasonable, don't hammer a domain, and only use this for legitimate outreach you're allowed to send. Sending unsolicited email is still subject to CAN-SPAM / GDPR / PECR.
